I discovered [this
talk](https://media.ccc.de/v/gpn22-410-my-files-are-a-mess-why-the-past-and-future)
titled "My files are a mess. Why??? The past and future" by
[dorotac](https://dorotac.eu/)[^fn:1].

Fascinating way to "accidentally" trip and fall on shoelaces as a nice
analogue to the premise of the talk. But, of course, the points raised
also resonate with me.

The question about if the photo metadata editor/searcher program is a
filesystem is very intriguing.

The talk goes into a brief history of hierarchical file systems, their
problems and what has been in development since then e.g. perkeep,
ipfs.

Very interesting talk overall and I plan to rewatch this properly
again with a fresher brain.
