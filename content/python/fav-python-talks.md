This is my ever-growing list of favorite python talks, in no particular
order.


## Raymond Hettinger {#raymond-hettinger}

Apart from the ones listed here, I would recommend everything by
Raymond Hettinger. He's a really good teacher and his talks are always
a pleasure to watch.
